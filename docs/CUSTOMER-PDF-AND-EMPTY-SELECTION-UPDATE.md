# Customer PDF and Customer Selection Update

- Customer directory PDF now shows only: #, Customer, Phone, Join date, Monthly rate, Current balance, and Status.
- Customer ID and Address have been removed from the PDF table. The overall customer and balance summary remains.
- Payments > Record a payment starts with an empty customer selection.
- Customer Ledger > Choose customer starts empty when opened from workspace navigation.
- Selecting a customer manually loads that customer's ledger. Opening Ledger from a specific customer row remains a deliberate preselection.
- Leaving the Ledger page clears that navigation context; clicking Ledger again while already there also resets the selection.
